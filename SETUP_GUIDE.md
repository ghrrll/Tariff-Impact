# Quick Setup Guide

## 5-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Create Google Sheet (1 minute)
1. Go to https://sheets.google.com
2. Click "Blank" to create a new spreadsheet
3. Name it "Grocery Price Tracker"

### Step 2: Add the Script (2 minutes)
1. In the sheet, click **Extensions** → **Apps Script**
2. Delete the default code
3. Copy everything from `GroceryPriceTracker.gs`
4. Paste into the editor
5. Click **Save** (💾 icon)
6. Close the Apps Script tab

### Step 3: Authorize & Setup (2 minutes)
1. Refresh your Google Sheet
2. Wait 10 seconds for the new menu to appear
3. Click **📊 Grocery Tracker** → **⚙️ Setup Sheets**
4. Click "OK" on the authorization prompt
5. Click "Advanced" → "Go to Grocery Price Tracker (unsafe)"
6. Click "Allow"
7. Done! All sheets are now created

## First Use

### Add Your First Entry
1. Click **📊 Grocery Tracker** → **➕ Add Weekly Entry**
2. Enter:
   - Your name: "John"
   - Store: "Walmart"
   - Total cost: "125.50"
   - Items: "30"
   - Notes: "Weekly shopping"
3. Click "Add Entry"

### View Results
- Check the **Store Analysis** sheet
- See your average cost per item automatically calculated

## Share with Others

1. Click the **Share** button (top right)
2. Add email addresses
3. Set to "Editor"
4. Click "Send"

Now others can add their data too!

## What's Next?

### Track More Data
- Add more weekly entries from different stores
- Try the **Line Item** tracker for specific products
- Get at least 2-3 weeks of data for meaningful comparisons

### View Analytics
- **Store Analysis**: Which store is cheapest overall?
- **Item Analysis**: Where to buy specific items?
- **Contributors Summary**: See who's contributing data and their stats

### Best Practices
- Enter data right after shopping (while fresh in memory)
- Use consistent store names (always "Walmart", not "walmart")
- Add notes about sales or special circumstances
- Update weekly for best trends

## Common Questions

**Q: Why isn't the menu showing?**
A: Refresh the page and wait 30 seconds. Close and reopen if needed.

**Q: Can I use this on mobile?**
A: Yes! Google Sheets works on mobile, and the forms adapt.

**Q: How many people can use this?**
A: Unlimited! Just share the sheet with Editor access.

**Q: Do I need to track line items?**
A: No, it's optional. Weekly entries alone are useful.

**Q: Where's my data stored?**
A: In your Google Sheet - you control it 100%.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Menu not appearing | Refresh page, wait 30 sec, try again |
| Authorization error | Click "Advanced" → "Go to... (unsafe)" |
| Form not saving | Check internet connection, fill all required fields |
| Wrong calculations | Click "Update All Analytics" from menu |
